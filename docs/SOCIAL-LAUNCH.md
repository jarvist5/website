# Leafivo social launch kit

These are draft materials for an owner to customize and review. Nothing here has been posted, scheduled, or purchased. Replace bracketed details with verified information, use your final store name, and link to your deployed HTTPS store. Do not advertise the sample products as available for purchase.

Working brand: **Leafivo**, pronounced “leaf-ee-voh.” Domain and trademark clearance are pending. The intended audience spans Pakistan, Uzbekistan, the United States, and India, with USD product pricing. Localize examples and language for each audience, and advertise only where your business can serve customers and process payments. Avoid “all cards accepted” or “available in every country” claims; acceptance must be verified with your eligible payment provider.

For a Pakistan-based merchant, complete the [Safepay eligibility and integration investigation](GO-LIVE.md#3-connect-and-test-payments) before announcing that purchases are open. Safepay is a potential provider, not an installed payment option in this store. Stripe checkout applies only to a legitimately eligible Stripe merchant.

## Offer and assets

Start with one real product and one specific customer problem. Write down: “For [audience], [product] helps with [specific task] through [actual contents].” Avoid promises of income, productivity gains, or transformation that you cannot substantiate.

Prepare a product cover, three actual inside-page screenshots you own, and a short screen recording of using the product. Avoid showing personal customer information. Use readable text, captions on video, descriptive alt text, and platform-licensed audio.

Create versions in these common formats, checking current platform specifications before posting:

| Format                        | Useful placement                                 |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1080 × 1350 image or carousel | Instagram and Facebook feed                      |
| 1080 × 1920 vertical video    | TikTok, Instagram/Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| 1000 × 1500 image             | Pinterest pin                                    |
| 1200 × 1200 image             | LinkedIn, Threads, X                             |

## Platform drafts

### Instagram / Facebook

**Five-card carousel:** (1) “A new chapter for your [specific task].” (2) A real problem your customer recognizes. (3) An actual page or tool with one useful tip. (4) What is included, format, and price. (5) Store name and a clear next step.

**Caption:**

> I made [product name] for [audience] who want a practical way to [task]. Inside: [three accurate contents]. Here is a look at [specific page/tool] and how I use it. Available as [format] for [price and currency]. See the full contents and license at [store link / link in bio]. What would you like to see inside before buying?

Use a few relevant topic hashtags, not unrelated trending tags. For Instagram, use an actual profile link or permitted story link; a feed-caption URL may not be clickable.

### TikTok

**20–30 second video:**

> 0–3 seconds: “If [specific task] keeps slipping off your list, try this.”
>
> 3–15 seconds: Show one real page from [product] and demonstrate one action. Explain what you are doing with captions.
>
> 15–23 seconds: “This is one part of [product], which includes [two actual features].”
>
> Final seconds: “See a preview and the full details at [store name]. [Link location, only if your account supports it.]”

**Caption:** “One small step for [task]. A look inside [product]. [Relevant topic hashtag].” Do not imply an endorsement by TikTok or use unlicensed music in business content.

### Pinterest

**Pin title:** “[Specific task]: a practical [ebook / planner / template]”

**Pin description:** “Explore [product], a [format] for [audience]. Includes [actual contents] to help you work through [task]. View sample pages, pricing, and license details at [store name].”

Use an original cover plus a readable sample-page detail. Link directly to the matching product, with a Pinterest-specific campaign tag.

### LinkedIn

> I built [product] after noticing [a real problem or personal experience]. It brings together [actual contents] in a [format] that [audience] can use for [specific task]. One idea from it: [useful standalone tip]. I am sharing a preview here, and the full product details are at [link]. I would value feedback on [specific question].

Publish from your own profile or business page with the appropriate access. Disclose your relationship to the product.

### YouTube Shorts

**Title:** “A quick way to approach [task] | Inside [product]”

**Script:** Show the finished example first, then two steps using your actual product. Close with: “The full [product] includes [contents]. Product details and preview: [accurate link location supported by your channel].”

**Description:** “[Product] is my [ebook / template] for [audience]. [Price and currency]. See contents and purchase terms at [store link].” Check current link eligibility and do not promise clickable links in placements that do not support them.

### Threads / X

> I have been working on [product]: a [format] for [audience] tackling [task]. It includes [two specific contents]. Here is one useful idea from it: [short tip]. Preview and details: [link].

Use a short thread only when each reply adds a helpful example. Reply to relevant questions thoughtfully; do not mass-message strangers or repeat the same promotion under unrelated posts.

## Seven-day organic launch

| Day | Work and publication draft                                                                      | What to learn                                                           |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Finalize the product page, policies, preview, and purchase test. Prepare platform-sized assets. | Can a new visitor understand what they receive and complete a purchase? |
| 2   | Share one useful tip on your strongest existing channel. Ask one relevant question.             | Which customer problem receives useful replies?                         |
| 3   | Publish an inside-page carousel on Instagram/Facebook and a matching Pinterest pin.             | Do people open the product detail or ask about contents?                |
| 4   | Record the short demonstration for TikTok/Reels/Shorts and tailor each caption.                 | Which demonstration holds attention?                                    |
| 5   | Share the creation story and a practical tip on LinkedIn; adapt it for Threads/X.               | Is the product reaching the intended audience?                          |
| 6   | Answer genuine questions. Update unclear product copy. Share an FAQ or another real example.    | What prevents a visitor from making an informed decision?               |
| 7   | Review channel visits, completed payments, refunds, and feedback. Plan the next week.           | Which content and channel justify more work?                            |

Choose the channels your customers use; you do not need to post everywhere on the first day. Obtain explicit consent before adding a person to marketing email. Connect an email service with unsubscribe handling before sending campaigns.

## Campaign links and measurement

Use a unique URL per platform, keeping personal information out of campaign parameters:

```text
https://YOUR_STORE_DOMAIN/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=leafivo_launch&utm_content=inside_pages
https://YOUR_STORE_DOMAIN/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=leafivo_launch&utm_content=demo_01
https://YOUR_STORE_DOMAIN/?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=leafivo_launch&utm_content=planner_pin
```

UTM tags label links; they do not install analytics. Configure an analytics service and any required consent before expecting traffic attribution. Until then, record platform-provided link clicks and confirmed payments separately. Do not equate clicks, cart additions, preview orders, or unpaid sessions with sales.

## Optional paid test — owner approval required

Do this only after real products, a functioning purchase/delivery flow, business policies, a billing account, and appropriate measurement are in place.

Proposed starting ceiling: **US$35 total**, one platform, one audience, **US$5 per day for seven days**. This is a proposal, not an authorized charge. Choose a currency-appropriate budget, verify current platform minimums and taxes, and approve the total cap before creating or launching a campaign. If a platform cannot enforce that cap, use a supported lifetime budget or do not launch it.

Use two truthful creatives featuring the same real product. Target the audience and countries you can serve. Review results after a meaningful interval instead of repeatedly changing settings. Pause at the approved cap; do not increase spend automatically. Measure paid orders, refund-adjusted revenue, and ad spend. A small test can produce no sales and may not provide enough data for a firm conclusion.

Connect advertising accounts and approve final creatives in the platform's own tools or an authorized integration. Never paste account passwords or payment credentials into project files or chat.
